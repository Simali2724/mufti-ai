# OCR model attribution

Tesseract traineddata is distributed under Apache 2.0. Models downloaded for this installation:
- Standard language packs: https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tessdata_fast
- High-quality Urdu: https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tessdata_best
- Nastaliq-specific urd_naw, contributed by Nawadiraat: https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tessdata_contrib/tree/main/urd_naw
- Tesseract license: https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tesseract/blob/main/LICENSE

This application does not claim perfect OCR accuracy. Keep and inspect the original PDF.
